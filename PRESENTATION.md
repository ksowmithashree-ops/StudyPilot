# StudyPilot — 3–4 minute presentation

## Problem

Students cram the wrong topics, overbook evenings, and never recover missed sessions.

## Demo script

1. Open the landing page. Show the SaaS UI and **Get Started**.
2. Dashboard: DBMS exam in 5 days, Normalization at 18%, low exam readiness.
3. Click **Generate AI Plan**. Normalization is first today. Total minutes stay within 3 hours.
4. Mark the Normalization session **Missed**. A toast and notification show the new slot.
5. Open Planner or Calendar to show the rescheduled session.
6. Complete a session or stay on Progress — readiness and charts update.
7. Optional: AI Assistant → “What should I study today?”
8. Close: adaptive + overload-safe, works without an API key.

## Talking points

- Priority score: deadline proximity, progress gap, difficulty, importance, missed boost, time fit.
- Overload detector caps each day to available minutes.
- Gemini refines notes/order when a key exists; scheduling math is always local.
